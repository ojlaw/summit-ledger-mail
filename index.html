Summit Ledger Mail

A private bookkeeping tool for one small business.

Summit Ledger Mail reads order confirmations, shipping notices and receipts from its owner's own Gmail accounts, so purchases can be matched to a bookkeeping ledger. It runs on the owner's own computer.

What it does

  • Reads email, read-only. It cannot send, change or delete mail.
  • Is used only by its owner, on the owner's own accounts.
  • Keeps what it reads on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold or shared.

Who can use it

This is not a public product. It is not offered to other people and there is nothing to sign up for.
